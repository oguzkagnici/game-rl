Winning

Dominion: ground units in the base's system with no Insurgent ground units left there.

Insurgents: time reaches reputation. Time starts at 1 and rises each round; reputation starts at 14 and falls when objectives are completed (one per round).

A round

  1. Assignment: mission cards face down with one or two leaders whose skill icons meet the card's need.
  2. Command: alternate turns, Insurgents first: reveal a mission, activate a system with a reserve leader, or pass.
  3. Refresh: one objective may be completed, the Dominion draws 2 probe cards, both sides spend build points, the queue deploys, leaders return, time advances, each side draws 2 mission cards and the Insurgents an objective. New leaders join at the start of rounds 3, 6 and 9.

Moving and fighting

Activating puts a leader in a system; your units from adjacent systems may move in, if the capital ships moving from each system can carry its fighters and ground units. Units under one of your leaders are pinned for the round.

Battles run in rounds until no domain is contested. Each round both sides may play a tactic card per domain, then roll: light dice hit fighters and light ground units on 4+, heavy dice hit capital ships and walkers on 4+, sixes hit anything. After a round, a side with a leader present may retreat to an adjacent system without enemies.

The hidden base

Base units sit off the map (counts are public). Moving them out reveals the base is in or next to that system. Dominion ground units or loyalty in its system reveal it; revealed, its bunkers cancel one hit on Insurgent ground units per round.

        Still simpler than the real game

        No action cards, a smaller unit roster, damage that is spread automatically, fixed recruit rounds, and one tactic deck per domain. Learn those details from the rulebook; this game is for the strategy.

        About the bots

        Both bots are scripted and show their reasoning. Balance was tuned with hundreds of bot-against-bot games: at Veteran level the Insurgents win roughly half, and games last 6 to 11 rounds.
