Rules used here

  • Each side has 12 stones. First you take turns dropping them on empty pits. No three in a row may be made while dropping.
  • Then you take turns sliding one stone one pit up, down, left or right.
  • A slide that makes exactly three of your stones in a row or column lets you take one enemy stone. Diagonals do not count.
  • Stones that sit inside an enemy three are safe, unless all of that side's stones are.
  • Four or more in a row is never allowed.
  • You lose when you are down to two stones or cannot move. After 200 actions the side with more stones wins.
