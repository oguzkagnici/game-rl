Play the self-taught network on any of the 8 terrains. Unofficial fan-made test page with its own drawings, not the game's artwork.

How to play, in four steps
  1. Each turn do one thing: draw 2 troops, or place 1 troop from your rack.
  2. To place: click a troop, then one of the glowing bases. You can only build outward from your H.Q. along the roads, through bases you hold.
  3. Covering: you may place on an empty base, on your own troop, or on an enemy troop with a lower number. Only the top troop of a pile counts.
  4. Win by placing a troop on the enemy H.Q., or by collecting enough gold medals. Hold every base around a medal to take it for good.

    About the model

    It does not see your rack. Before each search the tiles it cannot see are dealt at random, several times, and it picks the move that does best across those deals.

    House rulings where the rulebook leaves room: troop and base effects with a target can be skipped; Kwak passes every gate; Hook needs a normal link on Station Metal-X special bases; a game stops after 200 turns.

    Unofficial. Toy Battle is a game by Paolo Mori and Alessandro Zucchini, published by Repos Production. None of the game's artwork is used here. Terrain graphs follow a third-party transcription; the medal counts of Volcanic Jungle and Caribbean Sea are unverified.
